<p>Traders Fund Hub compares prop-firm challenges by their actual rules, not their headline account size. Each product row shows its price, profit target, drawdown method, profit split and payout conditions, and each figure links to the page on the firm's own website it came from.</p>

<h2>Where the numbers come from</h2>
<p>Each firm has one record for firm-level facts and a separate file for every challenge it sells. A figure goes into a product file only if it appears on the firm's own website, with that page's URL and the date we captured it. We never cite our own pages or a third-party review site as the source. If we can't verify a figure, we leave it empty and say so; we don't estimate it. A firm that prices in euros stays in euros, so a past exchange rate can't drift into the price.</p>

<h2>How we keep it current</h2>
<p>Prop-firm terms change often, so every product capture has a 30-day limit. A queue lists the oldest captures first. When one expires, the page flags it for a recheck and stops presenting it as current. To refresh a firm, we capture its pricing and rules pages again, and a script compares the new capture with the old one field by field. Any change to a price, tier, payout or rule has to be reviewed before it is published. An unfinished capture stays on hold until every open issue is resolved.</p>
<p>When a firm changes something that could affect a purchase, we add a dated entry to the public <a href="/prop-firm-challenge-changes">challenge change watch</a>. When two of a firm's own pages disagree, we keep the conflict visible and don't pick a winner. Country access is handled the same way: Russia, India, the UK and the US each have their own evidence file, with a dated quote from the firm's terms.</p>

<h2>How reviews are checked</h2>
<p>Firm reviews follow one fixed structure. An audit script checks every review before release: section order, banned marketing phrases, firm links that bypass our redirect, source age, and every stated fee against the product file. True-cost tables are generated from the product data by one function and are never typed in by hand. Our writing rules ask for a number, a named rule or a sourced claim in every paragraph, and for gaps to be stated rather than papered over.</p>

<h2>How the site is funded</h2>
<p>Some firm links are affiliate links. A partnership adds 0 points to a firm's score and doesn't change the product data or the source requirements. Partner firms may get more prominent placement and sign-up buttons; partner links are tagged as sponsored, and the page says we may earn a commission. <a href="/how-we-make-money">How we make money</a> lists which firms pay us and how the links work. The 0–10 score itself is explained in our <a href="/methodology">methodology</a>.</p>

<h2>When we get it wrong</h2>
<p>We fix the page and record the fix in the <a href="/corrections">corrections log</a>: the date, the page, what was wrong, the firm source and the commit. To report an error, send the page and the firm's source through the <a href="/contact">contact page</a>. Our writers are listed on the <a href="/authors">authors page</a>.</p>
