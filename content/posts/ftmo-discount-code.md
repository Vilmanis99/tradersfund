If you want the Instant Account, plan on list price.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The arithmetic that matters more than the deal</strong></h2>

<p>On the 2-Step, the deal mostly lowers the money you put at risk. FTMO's FAQ says the 2-Step fee is refunded "once you request your first Reward withdrawal". If you pass and get paid, you get back whatever you paid, €439 or €540. The €101 saving only stays with you if you fail. That still matters: <strong>two failed $100K 2-Step attempts at list price cost €1,080</strong>, the same as one $200K 2-Step.</p>

<p>On the 1-Step, the €100 saving is permanent, because the fee never comes back. Using our <a href="/true-cost-of-prop-firm-challenges">true-cost method</a> (fee ÷ split), the $100K 1-Step needs €554 of gross profit to earn back its €499 list fee at 90%. At €399 that falls to €443. That makes the 1-Step deal the bigger saving in practice. But the 1-Step has a 3% daily limit, a trailing floor and the Best Day rule, so pick the 1-Step for its rules, not for the €40 gap between the two deal prices.</p>

<p>Two limits follow from the wording. The deal covers only the $100K tier: the $50K 2-Step stays at €345 and the $200K at €1,080. And "only if you don't already have one active" means you can't stack two discounted $100K Challenges of the same product at once. FTMO doesn't say whether a reset or a new purchase after a failed attempt qualifies, so check the total at checkout.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Other ways FTMO lets you pay less</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Start with the Free Trial.</strong> FTMO's Free Trial costs nothing. FTMO's trial page, read on 23 Oct 2026, shows it runs 14 days on up to $200,000, with unlimited trials taken one at a time. It runs on MT4, MT5 and cTrader, and ends if you don't trade in the first 7 days. The 1-Step and 2-Step trials use a 5% target instead of 10%, and the 2-Step trial needs 2 minimum days instead of 4. There is also an Instant Account trial, with no profit target and a 30% consistency rule. Passing it doesn't fund you or discount a paid Challenge. Our <a href="/blog/ftmo-free-trial-explained">FTMO Free Trial guide</a> compares the trial rule sets in detail.</p>

<p><strong>Choose the refundable product if you expect to pass.</strong> The 2-Step's refund makes its fee a deposit for a trader who reaches the first reward. The 1-Step's lower fee is gone either way. At $50K, for example, that means €345 refundable against €319 not refundable.</p>

<p><strong>Buy the smallest size that tests your strategy.</strong> The $10K 2-Step costs €89 and has the same 10% and 5% targets and the same 10% static floor as the $100K. A failed €89 attempt teaches you the rules for a fraction of a $100K fee.</p>

<p><strong>Prime Status, later.</strong> FTMO's Premium Programme FAQ ("What are the benefits of Prime Status?", read 23 Oct 2026) lists a 10% discount on all new purchases, "not applicable for an FTMO Instant Account". It also lists one free FTMO Challenge of the same size, provided via FTMO Points, and access to a $400,000 Challenge with a 10% discount. You only get these after you qualify for Prime Status, so they don't help a first purchase.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Who the FTMO deal suits, and who should skip it</strong></h2>

<p><strong>It suits</strong> a trader who already planned to buy a $100K FTMO Challenge and has none active. On the 1-Step, the €100 is a saving you keep. On the 2-Step, the €101 lowers what you lose if you fail, and the rest comes back with your first reward.</p>
