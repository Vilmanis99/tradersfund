<!-- SECTION 4 - Rule mechanics -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>
<p><strong>EOD trailing drawdown moves after the session.</strong> A $50K Growth account begins with a $2,000 maximum-loss allowance and an initial $48,000 floor. If the end-of-day balance closes at $51,000, the next floor becomes $49,000; an intraday $51,500 peak that closes at $50,400 does not move the line to $49,500.</p>

<p><strong>The Growth DLL is a soft daily stop.</strong> A $50K Growth evaluation has a $1,250 daily limit and a separate $2,000 EOD trailing maximum loss. Reaching the $1,250 DLL stops trading for that day; crossing the maximum-loss floor breaches the account. Growth can pass in 1 day because its evaluation has no consistency rule.</p>

<p><strong>Select trades speed for intraday room.</strong> Its $50K evaluation has no DLL, a $2,000 EOD loss allowance and a 40% consistency rule. If the best day is $1,500 against the $3,000 target, consistency is 50% and the account needs at least $750 more profit before $1,500 becomes 40% of $3,750. This makes 3 profitable days the practical minimum.</p>

<p><strong>Select changes after the pass.</strong> Flex preserves no DLL and the $1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,500 loss allowances, then requires 5 winning days. Daily adds $500 / $1,000 / $1,250 / $1,750 DLLs and reduces the 100K and 150K loss allowances to $2,500 and $3,500, but it permits daily requests after the buffer.</p>

<p><strong>Lightning removes the evaluation, not the payout hurdles.</strong> A $50K Lightning account costs $492 and starts immediately with a $1,250 soft DLL and $2,000 EOD trailing loss. Its first payout needs $3,000 new profit and 20% consistency; the threshold becomes 25% for payout 2 and 30% from payout 3 onward.</p>

<p><strong>News is allowed, while microscalping is measured.</strong> Tradeify states no event blackout, but more than 50% of trades must last longer than 10 seconds and more than 50% of profit must come from those longer trades. Failing that two-part rule can block evaluation activation or payout eligibility without creating a separate news breach.</p>

<p><strong>Every product is intraday-only.</strong> Positions must close by 4:45 PM ET Monday-Friday, so overnight and weekend holds are prohibited. Same-direction copying is restricted to accounts the trader owns; hedging the same or correlated product across accounts is prohibited. Personal non-HFT automation is conditional on proving ownership, exclusive Tradeify use and live verification.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How much does a Tradeify account cost?</strong></h3>
<p>Growth list fees are $99, $145, $255 and $369; Select fees are $109, $165, $265 and $369; Lightning fees are $345, $492, $660 and $796 for $25K-$150K tiers. These are 2026-08-11 base purchase prices before temporary promotions; the separately published Select $50K reset fee was $109.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Does Tradeify charge monthly or activation fees?</strong></h3>
<p>No. The current Pricing Reference describes all 3 account types as one-time purchases with 0 recurring subscription charges, and Growth or Select activates the simulated funded stage for $0.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What is Tradeify's profit split?</strong></h3>
<p>The trader receives 90% of approved Growth, Select and Lightning simulated funded payouts. Elite Live uses an 80% trader split, so the 90% figure should not be projected onto the live stage.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Can Tradeify pay out daily?</strong></h3>
<p>Yes, on Select Daily after its buffer and continuity gates. The minimum request is $250, while the cap is $600 / $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,500 for $25K / $50K / $100K / $150K.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Does Tradeify allow news trading, EAs and copying?</strong></h3>
<p>News is allowed with no blackout, but the 50%-of-trades and 50%-of-profit microscalping test still applies. EAs are restricted to provably personal non-HFT systems, and copying is restricted to the trader's own accounts in the same direction.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Can Tradeify positions stay open overnight?</strong></h3>
<p>No. All Evaluation, Sim Funded and Live positions must close by 4:45 PM ET Monday-Friday, preventing overnight and weekend holding across all 4 captured paths.</p>
