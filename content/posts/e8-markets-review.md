<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>

<p><strong>E8 One — 6% trailing drawdown.</strong> E8 calls it Dynamic Drawdown: it "moves only when you close a profit" and "becomes static once you reach the starting balance amount." On a $100K account that is $6,000 of total room and a $4,000 daily limit, both hard breaches. Close $3,000 of profit and the floor climbs from $94,000 to $97,000, so the trailing mechanic tightens exactly as your account grows — until closed profit reaches 6% of starting balance, at which point the floor locks at $100,000.</p>

<p><strong>Unresolved conflict on E8 One's parameters.</strong> Two official E8 pages disagree. The help-centre article for E8 One describes the preset as "6% Profit Target… 3% Daily Drawdown… 4% Dynamic Drawdown," while the pricing page renders its default configuration as a 9% target, 4% daily drawdown and 6% max drawdown — the numbers used throughout this review, because they are the ones attached to the prices we captured. We have not reconciled the two; check which parameter set your order confirmation shows before you place a trade.</p>

<p><strong>E8 Pro — 8% static drawdown.</strong> "A fixed loss limit based on your initial balance. It never moves, except when a first payout is processed." On a $100K account that is a hard $92,000 floor for the whole evaluation, plus a 2.5% ($2,500) daily loss limit. The offsetting constraint is a <strong>2% daily profit cap</strong> in both stages: on a $100K account, a $5,000 day still counts only $2,000 toward the 8% target, so the fastest legal path to an $8,000 target is four qualifying days.</p>

<p><strong>E8 Signature — EOD trailing drawdown.</strong> The loss limit is "a moving loss limit based on your highest end-of-day balance," updated once per day at market close, so intraday equity swings do not move it. There is <em>no</em> daily loss limit during the challenge stage at all; on the Performance account a 2% daily pause stops trading for the day but explicitly does not breach the account. The trade-off is that Signature closes all positions at 23:00 server time and reopens at 00:15, and E8's own product comparison table marks both overnight and weekend holding as not allowed.</p>

<p><strong>Consistency rules differ by product.</strong> E8 Pro has none — "There is none - trade freely." E8 One applies a 40% best-day rule and E8 Signature a 35% best-day rule, in both cases meaning no single day may exceed that share of total generated profit. On E8 One the rule is scoped to the Performance account, not the challenge.</p>

<p><strong>Time limits and inactivity.</strong> All three products have unlimited trading days with no minimum, but every one carries the same dormancy clause: "you need to place and close at least one trade each 60 days." News trading is unrestricted on E8 Pro and E8 Signature; on E8 One it is unrestricted during the challenge but banned from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after high-impact releases on the Performance account. EAs are allowed at one strategy per user, and copy trading is allowed only between accounts you own.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>True cost to break even</strong></h2>

<p>Break-even profit is the fee divided by your profit split — the gain you must produce before your share of it repays what you spent. The R-multiple sets that figure against the dollar value of your maximum drawdown. R below 1 means the firm lets you lose more getting there than you need to make. <h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Payout speed in practice</strong></h2>

<p><strong>First payout.</strong> On E8 One and E8 Signature, "the earliest you can request a payout is 3 days after starting trading in the Performance stage," then on demand thereafter. <p><strong>Minimums and mechanics differ sharply by product.</strong> E8 One requires your net payout share to exceed 50% of your daily drawdown — E8's own worked example is a $100K account with a 4% daily drawdown, giving a $2,001 minimum request. E8 Signature's minimum is $100 net, which at an 80% split means requesting at least $125.</p>

<p><strong>E8 Signature carries payout caps and a buffer that the other two do not.</strong> The published cap table runs $1,000 ($25K), $1,250 ($50K), $2,250 ($100K) and $3,250 ($150K) on the first and second payouts, rising to $1,500, $3,250, $4,250 and $5,250 from the fifth payout onward. On top of that you must leave a non-requestable buffer equal to your EOD drawdown, and clear 5 profitable days between payouts after the first, where a profitable day means realised closed PnL of 0.3% or more.</p>
