<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Static and trailing loss room</strong></h3>
<p>On the standard 2-Phase Evaluation, a 10% static overall cap means an initial $100,000 account has a $90,000 overall floor. A profitable trade does not itself raise that fixed floor. <p>The 1-Phase programme starts with a 6% trailing allowance. <p>For Instant, the verified percentages are 4% daily and 6% overall. We have removed the unsupported “balance-based” classification rather than importing the 1-Phase trailing calculation. The percentage alone does not answer those questions.</p>
<p>The FAQ states that daily loss is referenced to the balance at 12:05 AM UTC and that equity is used for breaches. Open losses therefore matter. On the standard 2-Phase example, the 5% daily percentage and 10% overall percentage constrain different parts of the account's path.
