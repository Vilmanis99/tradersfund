If equity touches $104,000, even as floating profit, the floor moves up to $98,000; give that open profit back and the account breaches. The floor locks at the $100,000 starting balance only once equity reaches $106,000. On a $100K 2-Step Bright, the 8% floor stays at $92,000 regardless of how the account moves, which is why we point cost-sensitive buyers at the 2-Step plans first.</p>

<p><strong>The other fixed terms.</strong> The base funded split is 80%; 90% is a paid add-on and 100% is reached only through the scaling plan. The first payout request opens 30 days after the first funded trade, with later requests every 14 days, although BrightFunded's own payout article also describes bi-weekly payouts as an add-on, a wording conflict we still flag. There is no consistency rule, weekend and overnight holding are allowed, and funded accounts cannot open or close trades from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after listed high-impact news.</p>
