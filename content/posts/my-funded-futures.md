<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>
<p><strong>Rapid:</strong> the evaluation uses an end-of-day trailing line and a 6% target, then the Sim Funded stage switches to intraday trailing. The current rules table gives a $2,000 maximum loss and 5 mini/50 micro contracts for 50K; the floor locks at +$100 once the buffer is secured.</p>
<p>Rapid requires 2 evaluation days and a 50% evaluation-only consistency rule. Funded Rapid has no daily loss limit or consistency rule, but Tier 1 news trading is not allowed and the first payout depends on clearing the size-specific buffer rather than a fixed day count.</p>
<p><strong>Rapid EOD:</strong> the 25K and 50K articles set 4 minimum days, a 30% evaluation consistency rule, $1,000/$2,000 EOD trailing loss and no daily loss limit. The funded stage remains EOD trailing, needs a $1,100/$2,100 buffer, has a $500 minimum payout and uses a 90/10 split.</p>
<p><strong>Builder:</strong> the evaluation target is $1,500 on 25K and $3,000 on 50K, with a 1-day minimum and no evaluation consistency rule. The 50K default has a $2,000 EOD loss and a $1,000 soft pause; a soft pause ends the session, while a maximum-loss breach ends the account.</p>
<p>Builder's Sim Funded stage applies a 50% consistency rule at payout, requires 2 qualifying days per cycle, and caps each cycle at $1,000 on 25K or $2,000 on 50K after a $1,100/$2,100 buffer. Five approved simulated payouts trigger the documented live-account review.</p>
<p><strong>Pro:</strong> 50K, 100K and 150K use $2,000, $3,000 and $4,500 EOD maximum-loss values, 2 minimum days and a 50% evaluation-only consistency rule. Pro Sim Funded has no daily loss limit or funded consistency rule, but the first request is 14 calendar days from the first trade plus buffer clearance.</p>
<p>Across the current plan pages, overnight positions are not permitted. News permissions are stage-specific: evaluation news is generally allowed on Rapid EOD, Builder and Pro, while Tier 1 news is restricted or prohibited once the account is Sim Funded; EA and copy-trading permissions remain conditional, so obtain written plan-specific confirmation before automating.</p>
<p>The <a href="/blog/balance-based-drawdown-vs-equity-based-drawdown">drawdown guide</a> explains why Rapid's funded intraday trail and Rapid EOD's funded EOD trail are different risk surfaces. The <a href="/blog/what-is-prop-firm-consistency-rule">consistency guide</a> shows the 50% and 30% calculations with examples.</p>
