<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Verdict</strong></h2>
<p>My Funded Futures (MFF) is a futures prop firm selling one-time evaluations. A 50K My Funded Futures account costs $153 to $265 up front, but the number that decides your first payout is the buffer: $2,100 of realized profit on every 50K plan before a standard request. Rapid is the pick for an intraday futures trader who can build that buffer quickly and wants a request window 24 hours after the first funded trade; Builder suits a smaller-budget trader who accepts a per-cycle cap and a 50% payout consistency test; Pro only makes sense if you can wait 14 calendar days and want the highest ceiling. Russia-based traders can't buy a new account, because Russia is on MFFU's restricted-country list.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>MFF funded account rules</strong></h2>
<p>An MFF funded account is what MFFU calls Sim Funded: a simulated account you trade after passing the evaluation, with payouts paid from it. Rapid, Rapid EOD and Builder Sim Funded accounts start at a $0 balance, so the "buffer" is simply realized profit you must hold before any request. Four rules apply on every current plan; the table after them covers what changes by plan.</p>
<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Daily session close:</strong> MFFU's permitted-times article says "any open positions will be automatically closed at 4:10 PM EST on regular (non-holiday) trading days", and trading reopens with Globex at 6:00 PM. On holiday hours there is no auto-liquidation at the early close, and an open position left past the close breaches the account. Rapid's plan page adds: "Holding overnight will breach the account."</li>
  <li><strong>Weekends:</strong> no MFFU page we read uses the word "weekend". Our inference is that a weekend hold is impossible, because the daily 4:10 PM close also applies on Friday.</li>
  <li><strong>Inactivity:</strong> for one-time purchases from 25 August 2026, "If a trader does not place a trade for 7 consecutive calendar days, the account could be closed. This includes simulated funded accounts." MFFU asks traders to tell support before a break of more than 7 days. Builder's live account has no inactivity rule.</li>
  <li><strong>News:</strong> on Tier 1 releases (FOMC meetings, FOMC minutes, the Employment Report and CPI, plus EIA for energy and agricultural reports for ags), no position or order may be open from 2 minutes before to 2 minutes after the release. That restriction binds Rapid Sim Funded and Pro Sim Funded; MFFU's Rapid EOD plan page also marks Tier 1 news "Not allowed" when funded. Every evaluation and every Builder stage is unrestricted.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Rapid:</strong> MFFU's 50K article says your "first payout becomes available exactly 24 hours after your first trade on the Sim Funded account (as long as you meet the buffer and minimum)". The loss limit trails your equity high-water mark during the session at a fixed $2,000 distance on 50K. "Once your trailing Max Loss reaches $100, it locks there", which happens when the equity high reaches the $2,100 buffer.</p>
<p><strong>Rapid EOD:</strong> the loss line "does not move intraday – only at the close of each trading session". After the first payout, each new request needs $500 of net profit since the last one on 50K ($250 on 25K).</p>
<p><strong>Builder:</strong> every cycle needs 2 qualifying days, and your best day can't be more than 50% of the cycle's profit. The soft pause ends the session, not the account; only a maximum-loss breach closes it. A Builder 25K holder may run 2 accounts; the larger sizes allow 1 Builder account per user. After a live-account breach, a 21-day cooldown blocks Sim Funded trading and new purchases.</p>
<p><strong>Pro:</strong> the help article sets the MLL at $50,100, $100,100 or $150,100 after the first payout, where "it remains static". The live review is triggered by 3 consecutive payouts on the same account, reaching the $100,000 cap, an invitation, or $20,000 of profit, which "triggers a review only".</p>
<p>Our <a href="/blog/balance-based-drawdown-vs-equity-based-drawdown">drawdown guide</a> explains why Rapid's intraday trail and Rapid EOD's end-of-day trail are different risk surfaces, and the <a href="/blog/what-is-prop-firm-consistency-rule">consistency guide</a> shows the 50% and 30% calculations with examples.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Intraday vs EOD trailing: one day, two outcomes</strong></h3>
<p>Take a 50K funded account at $0. During the session an open trade reaches +$1,500, then you close the day at +$200. On Rapid, the line follows the $1,500 equity high and now sits at −$500, so you start tomorrow with $700 of room. On Rapid EOD, the line only moves on the closing balance, so it sits at −$1,800 and you keep $2,000 of room. That's an illustration with our numbers, applied to the mechanics each help article states. A trader who lets winners run and then give some back pays for Rapid's cheaper fee with a tighter line.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What it means to be an MFF funded trader</strong></h2>
<p>Passing doesn't put you on real capital. MFFU says "Upon completing your evaluation, you will be transitioned to a Sim Funded Account", a simulated account; only a Live Funded Account "involves a real brokerage account". The routes to Live are in the table's last row, and Rapid profit above $10,000 on the trigger day is forfeited. You can't decline ("rejecting the move to a Live Funded Account is not possible"), and your Sim Funded accounts are merged into one Live account. Live data, commissions and platform costs are "deducted directly from your Live Funded Account balance". Rapid Live pays 90/10 daily with no buffer and moves up to $5,000 of Sim Funded profit into a Reserve; Builder and Pro Live pay 80/20.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Does the MFF prop firm have a scaling plan?</strong></h3>
<p>Not a fixed one. MFFU's Live FAQ (updated November 2025, read 26 October 2026) says "there is no set scaling plan for our Live accounts" and that it works "one on one" on risk settings; you may ask for a limit review "once you have been live for 2 weeks". What MFFU publishes is the starting Live size: 3 minis or 30 micros on Rapid 50K, and 2, 3 or 4 contracts on Pro 50K, 100K and 150K. Merged Rapid accounts get a proportionally larger loss limit; contracts are then "discussed with the Live team".</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>MFF evaluation rules by plan</strong></h3>
<p>Each MFF evaluation is a one-time paid test before Sim Funded, recorded from MFFU's plan pages and help articles on 26 Oct 2026:</p>
<p><strong>Rapid:</strong> 6% target ($1,500 / $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000), EOD trailing loss, no daily loss limit, 2 minimum days and a 50% evaluation-only consistency rule. On a $3,000 pass, no day may contribute more than $1,500.</p>
<p><strong>Rapid EOD:</strong> 25K and 50K only, with $1,000 / $2,000 EOD trailing loss, 4 minimum days and a 30% evaluation consistency rule. On a $3,000 pass, the best day is capped at $900.</p>
<p><strong>Builder:</strong> 1 minimum day and no evaluation consistency rule, so a single $3,000 day can pass a 50K. The soft daily pause is $600 / $1,000 / $1,750 / $2,500 by size.</p>
<p><strong>Pro:</strong> 50K, 100K and 150K with $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,500 EOD maximum loss, 2 minimum days and a 50% evaluation-only consistency rule. Targets are $3,000 / $6,000 / $9,000, and no daily loss limit applies.</p>
