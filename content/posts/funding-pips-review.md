<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>

<p><strong>1 Step Flex:</strong> the 12% static maximum loss stays anchored to starting balance while the daily cap is 3%. On a $100K account, those percentages represent $12,000 total room and $3,000 in one day; a 12% target means the target and total loss allowance are equal.</p>

<p><strong>2 Step Flex:</strong> the targets are 10% and 6% against a 4% daily and 12% static maximum loss. The buyer chooses an 85% split with no minimum profitable-day condition or a 95% split that requires 3 profitable days of at least 0.5% in each payout cycle.</p>

<p><strong>2 Step Pro:</strong> both phases target 6%, the daily cap is 3%, and the static maximum loss is 6%. It requires 1 minimum trading day and pays an 80% split; the former Risk Per Trade Idea rule is explicitly marked removed in the captured article.</p>

<p><strong>2 Step Standard:</strong> targets are 8% then 5%, with a 5% daily and 10% static maximum loss plus 3 minimum trading days. Its 35% consistency rule applies only when the trader chooses the 90% on-demand payout cycle, not during evaluation or on the other 3 payout cycles.</p>

<p><strong>FundingPips Zero:</strong> there is no evaluation target, but the funded account uses a 3% daily and 5% trailing maximum loss. The first 3% profit is a safety cushion, reward eligibility needs 7 profitable days of at least 0.25% in a rolling 30-day period, and the largest losing day cannot exceed the largest winning day.</p>

<p><strong>Trading permissions are restricted, not universally allowed.</strong> The 4 evaluation products mark news, weekend, overnight, EA, and copy trading as restricted. Zero rejects news and weekend trading outright and keeps overnight, EA, and copy trading restricted; traders should read the named restriction instead of treating “restricted” as either “yes” or “no.”</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>True cost to break even</strong></h2>

FundingPips supplies 4 of those inputs on some products but supplies 0 verified fees across all 29 rows, so no break-even, R-multiple, or realistic-day table can be generated without inventing the missing price.</p>

<p>The honest buying sequence is therefore 2 steps: choose the rule set first, then record the live checkout fee in its published currency. Until that value enters the source capture, Traders Fund Hub will not label FundingPips “cheap” or compare a temporary coupon against a verified rival list price.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Payout speed in practice</strong></h2>

<p>1 Step Flex and 2 Step Flex publish a first reward after 14 days and a bi-weekly cycle. 2 Step Pro shortens both to 7 days and weekly, while Zero returns to 14 days and bi-weekly.</p>

<p>2 Step Standard offers 4 distinct choices: 90% on demand with a 35% consistency score and 2% minimum profit, 60% weekly after 7 days, 80% bi-weekly after 14 days, or 100% monthly after 30 days. Quoting one “FundingPips split” for this product would erase the payout-speed trade-off.</p>

<p>The aggregate firm record lists bank wire, Rise, and crypto. It does not yet contain a dated India-specific availability, KYC, or payout-rail capture, so an Indian buyer should not assume all 3 methods will appear after verification.</p>

<p>Trustpilot showed 4.5/5 from 63,734 reviews on 2026-07-27, but the site’s verified-payout badge remains false. A large review aggregate is community evidence; it is not proof that one strategy, KYC profile, or payout request will clear the firm’s rules.</p>
