<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>

<p><strong>Intraday Trail follows unrealized peaks.</strong> A $50K evaluation starts with a $2,000 maximum loss, placing the first threshold at $48,000. If open equity reaches $51,000, the real-time trailing rule can lift the threshold to $49,000 before the trade closes; giving the $1,000 gain back does not move the threshold down.</p>

<p><strong>EOD Trail updates from the closing balance.</strong> The same $50K size has a $2,000 maximum loss, but the next-session threshold is calculated after market close. A $51,000 end-of-day balance implies a $49,000 threshold for the following session, so an intraday spike that disappears before the close does not tighten the line in the same way.</p>

<p><strong>EOD has a separate daily-loss pause.</strong> Evaluation DLLs are $500 on $25K, $1,000 on $50K, $1,500 on $100K and $2,000 on $150K. Touching the DLL closes positions and pauses trading until the 6:00 PM ET reset; it does not fail the evaluation. Touching the EOD maximum-loss threshold does fail it.</p>

<p><strong>The 50% consistency rule starts after passing.</strong> Apex applies no evaluation consistency percentage, but a PA payout is unavailable if the largest profitable day is 50% or more of net profit since inception or the last approved payout. A $900 best day on $2,000 total profit is 45% and passes that test; a $1,000 best day is exactly 50% and does not.</p>

<p><strong>Every trade must close before 4:59 PM ET.</strong> The day-trading rule allows new orders again at 6:00 PM ET but excludes overnight and weekend positions. News trading is restricted: a normal strategy may trade during a release, while two-sided orders, news chasing and hedging to gamble on the outcome are prohibited.</p>

<p><strong>Automation is banned, while personal copying is conditional.</strong> Bots, scripts, algorithms and preprogrammed order logic are prohibited. The User Agreement permits copying only when 1 manually controlled originating account receives the trader's real-time order and the same user's receiving accounts mirror that order exactly without alteration.</p>
