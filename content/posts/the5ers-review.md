<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Verdict</strong></h2>

<p>The5ers sells four programs, not one challenge. The cheapest ticket is $19 for a $2.5K High Stakes account and the dearest is $850 for a $20K Hyper Growth account, and they come with very different contracts. Pick The5ers only if you want High Stakes' 80% split that scales toward 100%, a 3-profitable-day minimum and no time limit, and you can live with MT5 as the only platform and a payout clock that starts 14 days after funding.</p>

<p>The data below comes from The5ers' own program pages and Terms, captured on 2026-10-19. Two things on those pages don't line up, and we flag both instead of picking a side: the refund promise in the High Stakes table, and the "no daily stop-loss" header on the Growth programs.</p>

<div class="key-takeaways">
  <div class="title">Fast take</div>
  <ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>High Stakes: New or Classic</strong></h3>

<p>Both versions are two steps with no time limit, the same loss limits and the same 5% second-step target. The only difference is step 1. New asks for 10% and is cheaper, while Classic asks for 8% and costs more. At $100K that means paying $50 more ($455 against $405) to need $8,000 of step-1 profit instead of $10,000. If your average month on a $100K account is closer to 8% than 10%, Classic is the honest choice. The $50 is less than one failed retake.</p>

<p>Passing step 1 also pays a small fixed reward of $2, $5, $10, $15, $25 or $40, depending on the tier. Don't count it as income. On the $100K tier, $40 is under 10% of the $405 fee.</p>

<p><strong>The gotcha is the tier break at $50K.</strong> Up to $25K the limits are 5% daily and 10% total. On $50K and $100K they drop to 4% and 8%. On $100K that is a $4,000 daily line and an $8,000 account line, not the $5,000 and $10,000 a trader moving up from a $25K account might expect. Size positions off the 4% figure from day one.</p>

<p>The5ers' pages don't say whether the maximum loss is measured from the starting balance (static) or moves with the account. The table states a percentage of the initial balance, so treat it as static until The5ers confirms it in writing. Our <a href="/blog/balance-based-drawdown-vs-equity-based-drawdown">balance vs equity drawdown guide</a> shows how much the difference can cost on an open losing trade.</p>

<p><strong>News:</strong> on High Stakes, opening or closing trades 2 minutes before or after high-impact news is not allowed. <strong>Weekend and overnight:</strong> holding is allowed, but indices carry a high weekend swap. <strong>Consistency:</strong> we found no consistency rule on the program page on 2026-10-19. That is "not found", not "confirmed absent", so ask support before you build a strategy around one big day.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Pro Growth and Hyper Growth</strong></h3>

<p>Both Growth programs are one step with a 10% target, a 6% stop-out level, 1:30 leverage and no time limit. Pro Growth needs 3 profitable days and costs $52 to $329. Hyper Growth has no minimum days and costs about four to five times as much: $450 for a $10K account against $98 on Pro Growth. Hyper Growth doubles the funded account each time you hit a 10% target.</p>

<p><strong>Gotcha:</strong> the page header says "No daily stop-loss", but the rendered table shows a 3% daily limit, labelled "Daily Pause" in the page data. Treat 3% as live. On a $20K Growth account that is $600 in one day.</p>

<p>Two more Growth details need a check before you buy. The profit split is published only as "up to 100%", and the scaling table starts at 75%, without saying which Growth version it belongs to. The page also caps combined evaluation capital at $40,000 per trader while listing a $50K Pro Growth tier. Ask The5ers which applies before you buy the $50K tier or stack accounts.</p>

<p>News trading is allowed on Growth, except for bracket strategies around news and the practices listed in the Terms.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Bootcamp</strong></h3>

<p>Bootcamp swaps one fee for two. You pay a small entry fee, pass three 6% steps with a 5% maximum loss and no daily limit, and then pay a second fee to activate the funded account. On the $100K plan that is $95 now and $205 later, $300 in total if you pass. The funded stage then tightens to a 4% maximum loss and a 3% daily pause, with a 5% target. The Bootcamp table also says each active account must use a different strategy.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Rules that apply across programs</strong></h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Copy trading is prohibited.</strong> The Terms ban "trade coordination or copy trading with other traders or accounts". See <a href="/blog/what-is-copy-trading">what copy trading means at prop firms</a> before you mirror signals.</li>
  <li><strong>EAs are allowed with exceptions.</strong> The Terms prohibit EAs that scalp the rollover to exploit the price feed, third-party EAs whose trades other traders also hold, and EAs whose source code you don't own.</li>
  <li><strong>Refunds are unresolved.</strong> The High Stakes table shows "Refund" at the Pro Trader stage, but the Terms say evaluation fees are non-refundable once trading starts. Don't count on getting the fee back until The5ers explains the condition.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Payout speed in practice</strong></h2>

<p>The rule is the same in every program. The first payout comes 14 days after you receive the funded account (on Growth, after it is activated), and then every 2 weeks. None of the captured program pages lists an on-demand option or a paid add-on for a faster first payout. Plan cash flow on a fortnightly cycle.</p>

<p>The High Stakes split starts at 80% and scales to 100% through the scaling plan. The Growth and Bootcamp pages publish only "up to 100%". Our <a href="/blog/prop-firm-scaling-plan">prop firm scaling plan guide</a> covers what usually triggers each step.</p>

<p>Payout methods are the gap. The5ers' help-centre payout articles returned 404 on 2026-10-19, so we have no first-party list of methods. Confirm your withdrawal method with support before you pay for an evaluation.</p>

<p>On Trustpilot, The5ers showed 4.7 from 40,175 reviews on 2026-10-20, with 21,958 of them from the last 12 months. That is customer opinion, not proof of payouts. Our verified-payout badge for The5ers is still off.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Pros</strong></h2>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
