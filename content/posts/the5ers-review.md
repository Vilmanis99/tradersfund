<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Verdict</strong></h2>

<p>The5ers sells four standard programs, not one challenge, plus a limited-time Summer Plan. The cheapest standard ticket is $19 for a $2.5K High Stakes account and the dearest is $850 for a $20K Hyper Growth account. Pick The5ers only if you want High Stakes' 80% split that scales toward 100%, a 3-profitable-day minimum, no time limit and a static loss line, and you are fine with a payout clock that starts 14 days after funding and a 3.5% fee on every withdrawal.</p>

<p>The standard-program data comes from The5ers' program pages, FAQ and Terms, rechecked on 2026-10-20. Summer Plan prices are the firm's advertised list prices from the same day. We could not verify them at checkout, which needs a sign-in.</p>

<div class="key-takeaways">
  <div class="title">Fast take</div>
  <ul>
    <li>The 2026-10-20 capture covers 5 standard products with 22 priced tiers: High Stakes New, High Stakes Classic, Pro Growth, Hyper Growth and Bootcamp.</li>
    <li>High Stakes New costs $405 for $100K with a 10% then 5% target; Classic costs $455 for the same size with an 8% then 5% target.</li>
    <li>On the $50K and $100K High Stakes tiers the loss limits tighten to 4% daily and 8% total, against 5% and 10% on the $2.5K–$25K tiers.</li>
    <li>Drawdown is static on every program: the maximum loss is a fixed line set from the initial balance.</li>
    <li>The first withdrawal comes 14 days after funding, then every 2 weeks, with a $150 minimum. Rise, crypto and bank transfer each charge 3.5%.</li>
    <li>High Stakes' "Refund" is a passing reward, not a cash-back guarantee. Pro Growth, Hyper Growth and Bootcamp fees are non-refundable.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>High Stakes: New or Classic</strong></h3>

<p>Both versions are two steps with no time limit, the same loss limits and the same 5% second-step target. The only difference is step 1. New asks for 10% and is cheaper, while Classic asks for 8% and costs more. At $100K that means paying $50 more ($455 against $405) to need $8,000 of step-1 profit instead of $10,000. If your average month on a $100K account is closer to 8% than 10%, Classic is the honest choice. The $50 is less than one failed retake.</p>

<p>Passing step 1 also pays a small fixed reward of $2, $5, $10, $15, $25 or $40, depending on the tier. Don't count it as income. On the $100K tier, $40 is under 10% of the $405 fee.</p>

<p><strong>The gotcha is the tier break at $50K.</strong> Up to $25K the limits are 5% daily and 10% total. On $50K and $100K they drop to 4% and 8%. On $100K that is a $4,000 daily line and an $8,000 account line, not the $5,000 and $10,000 a trader moving up from a $25K account might expect. Size positions off the 4% figure from day one.</p>

<p><strong>Drawdown is static.</strong> The5ers' drawdown FAQ describes the maximum loss as a fixed line from the initial balance ("absolute drawdown"). The daily limit is measured from the higher of the previous day's closing equity or balance. On a $100K account the $92,000 floor stays put however far the account grows; open trades count against both lines. Our <a href="/blog/balance-based-drawdown-vs-equity-based-drawdown">balance vs equity drawdown guide</a> explains why the "higher of equity or balance" detail matters when you hold winners overnight.</p>

<p><strong>News:</strong> on High Stakes, opening or closing trades 2 minutes before or after high-impact news is not allowed. <strong>Weekend and overnight:</strong> holding is allowed, but indices carry a high weekend swap.</p>

<p><strong>Consistency:</strong> none is listed on the program pages for High Stakes, Growth or Bootcamp (rechecked 2026-10-20). The5ers' FAQ adds a caveat without naming accounts: "Some accounts may have a 30% Consistency Rule, while others may have a 50%." If your strategy depends on one big day, ask support which figure, if any, applies to your account before you buy.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Pro Growth and Hyper Growth</strong></h3>

<p>Both Growth programs are one step with a 10% target, a static 6% stop-out level, 1:30 leverage and no time limit. Pro Growth needs 3 profitable days and costs $52 to $329. Hyper Growth has no minimum days and costs about four to five times as much: $450 for a $10K account against $98 on Pro Growth. Hyper Growth doubles the funded account each time you hit a 10% target.</p>

<p>The funded split starts at 75% on both. On the published scaling table it stays at 75% up to a $300,000 account, rises to 80% at $350,000 and reaches 80%–100% from $400,000. In practice, you should price a Growth account at 75%.</p>

<p>Account limits differ. Hyper Growth caps combined evaluation capital at $40,000 per trader, for example one $20K, one $10K and two $5K accounts. Pro Growth instead allows up to 4 accounts, one of each size, and only Pro Growth has the $50K tier ($329).</p>

<p><strong>Gotcha:</strong> the page header says "No daily stop-loss", but the rendered table shows a 3% daily limit, labelled "Daily Pause" in the page data. Treat 3% as live. On a $20K Growth account that is $600 in one day. News trading is allowed on Growth, except for bracket strategies around news and the practices listed in the Terms.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Bootcamp</strong></h3>

<p>Bootcamp swaps one fee for two. You pay a small entry fee, pass three 6% steps with a static 5% maximum loss and no daily limit, and then pay a second fee to activate the funded account. On the $100K plan that is $95 now and $205 later, $300 in total if you pass. The funded stage then tightens to a 4% maximum loss and a 3% daily pause, with a 5% target. Each active account must use a different strategy. The funded split is published only as "up to 100%", so we can't price Bootcamp's true cost.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Summer Plan rules</strong></h3>

<p>Every Summer account has a 3% daily loss, set at the end of each day from the higher of equity or balance. Hitting it terminates the account permanently. The maximum loss is static: 6% on the 1-Step and 10% on the 2-Step. The 1-Step has a 10% target, 1:100 leverage, a 75% split after passing, a limit of 2 active accounts, and a 50% consistency rule in both evaluation and funded stages. The 2-Step has no consistency rule during evaluation and a 50% rule once funded, with an 80% split.</p>

<p>On $100K, the 50% rule means a single $5,000 day needs $10,000 of total profit before a payout. Payouts are capped at $1,000 per cycle on $50K, $2,000 on $100K and $3,000 on $200K, with a $250 minimum. The Summer Plan FAQ (read 2026-10-29) allows up to 2 × 1-Step, 2 × 2-Step 10%/5% and 2 × 2-Step 8%/5% $50K accounts at once, subject to the $600,000 total Summer buying-power cap; the $200K accounts don't scale. Copying trades between your own Summer accounts is allowed, but copying from third-party accounts is not.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Rules that apply across programs</strong></h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Copy trading with others is prohibited.</strong> The Terms ban "trade coordination or copy trading with other traders or accounts". See <a href="/blog/what-is-copy-trading">what copy trading means at prop firms</a> before you mirror signals.</li>
  <li><strong>EAs are allowed with exceptions.</strong> The Terms prohibit EAs that scalp the rollover to exploit the price feed, third-party EAs whose trades other traders also hold, and EAs whose source code you don't own.</li>
  <li><strong>Refunds depend on the program.</strong> The Terms give a full refund within 5 days of purchase if you haven't traded, and Hub Credit only after 5 days. Once you trade, the fee is non-refundable. The Pro Growth, Hyper Growth and Bootcamp fees are non-refundable. The Oct. 28th 2026 Terms update adds that eligible refunds for Latin American local or alternative payment methods are issued solely as Hub Credits (read 2026-10-29), provided no trading has started.</li>
  <li><strong>High Stakes' "Refund" is a passing reward.</strong> You get 10% of the fee as Hub Credit at step 1 and 20% at step 2. The other 70% is paid with your first payout once funded, provided you have made at least $150 profit and the account has been active for 14 days. It applies only to fees paid with external funds, not Hub Credit.</li>
  <li><strong>Summer 2-Step works the same way</strong>, 10% and 20% in Hub Credit, but the 70% comes as cash alongside the 3rd payout. The 1-Step table says only "Refund From 3rd Payout".</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>True cost to break even</strong></h2>

<p>The tables below come from <code>computeTrueCost()</code> and The5ers' 2026-10-20 list fees. Break-even profit is the fee divided by the starting split: 80% on High Stakes and 75% on Pro Growth and Hyper Growth. The R-multiple compares that profit with the maximum loss: 10% up to $25K on High Stakes, the $4,000 or $8,000 limits on $50K and $100K, and 6% on Growth. Days assume 1% a day. The 3.5% withdrawal commission is not included.</p>

<p>Worked example: a $100K High Stakes New account costs $405. To earn the fee back you need $506 of funded profit at 80%, about 0.5% of the account. That is 0.06 of the $8,000 loss limit, or one day at 1%. The fee is the small number. The hard part is the 15% of combined targets you have to clear first. Hyper Growth is the opposite case: a $10K account needs $600 of profit just to cover its $450 fee, which is 1.00 of its $600 stop-out.</p>
