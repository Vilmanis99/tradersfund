<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Verdict</strong></h2>

<p>Hash Hedge sells 16 priced challenges, from $79 for a $5K account to $1,393 for $200K, and you trade them on the firm's own web terminal rather than MT5. Pick it only if you want 24/7 crypto perpetuals with no time limit and positions you can hold over a weekend, and you accept that the firm's own pages disagree on drawdown, payment methods and its maximum account size.</p>

<p>Every rule and price below comes from Hash Hedge's own pages. Prices were first captured on 2026-10-19 and re-read unchanged on 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-26. The Terms and Commercial Terms were re-read on 2026-10-31 and are dated "Last Updated: October 30, 2026". The user guide and the homepage carry no page date. We did not sign up or reach checkout, and no payout has been independently verified.</p>

<div class="key-takeaways">
  <div class="title">Fast take</div>
  <ul>

    <li>Overnight and weekend holding depends on the product: allowed on Crypto + TradFi, barred on US Stocks.</li>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Loss limits, with a worked example</strong></h3>

<p>The guide measures percentages against account size. On a $25,000 Two-stage account, Stage 1 asks for $1,250 (5% per the table; a card says "+8%"), with a $1,250 (5%) daily and $2,500 (10%) total loss. Stage 2 targets 6% ($1,500) with total loss at 8% ($2,000); funded keeps 5% daily and 8% total. Daily loss is "a fixed 5% of your Challenge's starting balance". On Crypto + TradFi it resets 00:00–00:13 UTC+4 (guide FAQ, unchanged 2026-10-26).</p>

<p>One-stage is tighter: a 10% target ($2,500 on $25K) against a 3% daily ($750) and 6% total ($1,500) limit, with no stage-2 step-down. Open positions count toward the limits, so holding through a weekend does not pause them.</p>

<p>We cannot say whether the 10% and 8% total limits are static or trailing. The guide calls total loss "the overall account loss level that, once reached, ends the Challenge" with no base formula; the homepage labels it "Peak-to-trough loss limit across the full challenge". Those readings differ. See our <a href="/blog/balance-based-drawdown-vs-equity-based-drawdown">drawdown basis guide</a>. Ask support in writing before you buy.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Overnight, weekends and news: it depends on the product</strong></h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Crypto + TradFi allows overnight and weekend holding.</strong> The FAQ says "On a Crypto + TradFi account, yes: just watch the loss limits, because open positions count toward them" and "We don't restrict trading during news events or on weekends."</li>
  <li><strong>US Stocks bars both.</strong> Hash Hedge's own user guide (stock terminal page, read 2026-10-23) says "You cannot hold a stock position overnight, over a weekend, or for several days". New positions are blocked from 23:55 UTC+4 and every open position closes at 23:59 UTC+4.</li>
</ul>

<p>A single overnight answer would be wrong here. Holding for days means Crypto + TradFi only. See <a href="/best-swing-trading-prop-firms">swing trading prop firms</a>.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Trading costs, with a worked example</strong></h3>

<p>Hash Hedge charges maker 0.01% and taker 0.03% "on the total position size, including leverage, both when you open and when you close". Funding is variable and "exchanged between traders, not paid to the platform". Leverage is capped at 1:5 on every stage (homepage and guide, 2026-10-26); the "LEVERAGE X10" banner from 2026-10-23 is gone. A $100,000 notional (4x on $25K) costs $60 round trip at the taker rate, or $20 at maker. No perpetuals spread is published. US Stocks: 0.04% with a $1.00 minimum (user guide, 2026-10-23).</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Rules that apply across products</strong></h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>No time limit.</strong> The guide says a challenge "has no deadline".</li>
  <li><strong>Automation is out.</strong> Terms 12.2 bars "automated tools, bots, scripts, APIs, or software" that interact with the platform, and the platform is Hash Hedge's own terminal, so there is no MT4/MT5 EA route.</li>
  <li><strong>Hedging has a boundary.</strong> Holding long and short in the same instrument inside one account is permitted, but "Prohibited Hedging" means opposing positions across two or more challenges or accounts. Arbitrage and manipulation are banned.</li>
  <li><strong>No consistency rule found.</strong> We found none in the guide, Terms or Commercial Terms. That is not confirmation that none exists.</li>
  <li><strong>Identity checks come later.</strong> The guide says "there's no KYC" at purchase, but the Commercial Terms require identity verification before a Live Account and allow enhanced Re-Verification as a condition of any payout.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Payout speed in practice</strong></h2>

<p>On a Live Account, a payout needs an eligible profitable trading cycle: profit, closed positions, and the selected payout day. Commercial Terms (Last Updated: October 30, 2026, checked 2026-10-31) allow successive Live Account trading cycles under the same Challenge while the account stays active, with no more than one Live Account reward payout per cycle. Completing a cycle does not, by itself, close the Live Account.</p>

<p>Minimum payout and cycle length still conflict. The user guide (Profit Share and Payouts, checked 2026-10-31) states a $100 minimum, a $10,000 maximum per account per 14 days, and "Every 14 days", with a new request no earlier than 14 days after the last completed one. Commercial Terms set the minimum at USD 1, keep the $10,000 maximum per fourteen calendar days, and end each cycle on a payout day you select "between fourteen (14) and sixty (60) calendar days from the opening of the first trade in that trading cycle". We do not treat the minimum or a fixed 14-day cycle as settled—day 14 is the earliest. Withdrawals are USDT on six networks (ERC-20, TRC-20, BEP-20, Arbitrum, Optimism, Solana); the homepage says Hash Hedge "sends the exact amount and covers the network fee".</p>

<p>Two-stage Challenges also carry a First-Stage Performance Bonus. Commercial Terms 1.3 (30 Oct 2026) pays 2% of the first-stage profit target after Stage 1 is confirmed passed, "payable only once per Challenge", irrespective of profit actually made—not a Live Account split. The guide's Stage 1 Reward page (checked 2026-10-31) matches the 2% figure ($10 on $10K; $100 on $100K at a 5% Stage 1 target) and says One-stage Challenges have no reward. Claim timing conflicts: the guide says an unclaimed reward "expires" if you reach Trader without claiming; the Commercial Terms describe Stage 2 and later Live-cycle windows. Treat 2% once-per-Challenge as confirmed and claim timing as unsettled.</p>

<p>On timing, the guide says funds are transferred "within 72 hours of submitting your withdrawal request". That is a ceiling, not an average, and Hash Hedge publishes no average payout time or payout statistics. The Commercial Terms also describe a payout processing guarantee: if a valid, complete request is not processed and paid within 72 hours, you are entitled to three times the payout. The guarantee applies only to requests "not subject to any Hold, investigation, Re-Verification or enhanced identity verification requirement". If one of those arises after submission, the 72-hour clock stops and a fresh 72 hours starts in full once the request is valid again. It also stops for delays outside the firm's reasonable control, including payment-network processing times and incorrect or unverified payout details. That makes the guarantee conditional on a clean request, which is a weaker promise than the homepage line "If your payout is delayed by more than 72 hours, we pay you triple".</p>
