<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Verdict</strong></h2>

<p>Hash Hedge sells 16 priced challenges, from $79 for a $5K account to $1,393 for $200K, and you trade them on the firm's own web terminal rather than MT5. Pick it only if you want 24/7 crypto perpetuals with no time limit and positions you can hold over a weekend, and you accept that the firm's own pages disagree on drawdown, payment methods and its maximum account size.</p>

<p>Every rule and price below comes from Hash Hedge's own pages, read on 2026-10-23 and re-read on 2026-10-26 (prices were first captured on 2026-10-19 and unchanged on both later reads). The Terms and Commercial Terms are dated "Last Updated: October 8, 2026". The user guide and the homepage carry no page date. We did not sign up or reach checkout, and no payout has been independently verified.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How the rules actually work</strong></h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Loss limits, with a worked example</strong></h3>

<p>The guide states that all percentages are measured against your account size. On a $25,000 Two-stage account, Stage 1 asks for $1,250 (5%, per the homepage table; a card says "+8%"), the daily loss limit is $1,250 (5%) and the maximum total loss is $2,500 (10%). In Stage 2 the target is 6% ($1,500) and the total loss limit tightens to 8% ($2,000), and the funded Trader stage keeps 5% daily and 8% total. The daily limit is "a fixed 5% of your Challenge's starting balance", so it does not move with your running balance. On Crypto + TradFi it resets between 00:00 and 00:13 UTC+4 (guide FAQ, read 2026-10-19, unchanged 2026-10-26).</p>

<p>One-stage is tighter: a 10% target ($2,500 on $25K) against a 3% daily ($750) and 6% total ($1,500) limit, with no stage-2 step-down. Open positions count toward the limits, so holding through a weekend does not pause them.</p>

<p>We cannot tell you whether the 10% and 8% total limits are static or trailing. The guide's total-loss page defines the limit only as "the overall account loss level that, once reached, ends the Challenge", with no formula for its base. The homepage selector labels the same line "Peak-to-trough loss limit across the full challenge". Those two readings differ in practice: a peak-based line rises as you profit, while a line measured from your starting size does not. Our <a href="/blog/balance-based-drawdown-vs-equity-based-drawdown">balance-based vs equity-based drawdown guide</a> shows why the base matters. Ask support in writing before you buy.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Overnight, weekends and news: it depends on the product</strong></h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>Crypto + TradFi allows overnight and weekend holding.</strong> The FAQ says "On a Crypto + TradFi account, yes: just watch the loss limits, because open positions count toward them" and "We don't restrict trading during news events or on weekends."</li>
  <li><strong>US Stocks bars both.</strong> Hash Hedge's own user guide (stock terminal page, read 2026-10-23) says "You cannot hold a stock position overnight, over a weekend, or for several days". New positions are blocked from 23:55 UTC+4 and every open position closes at 23:59 UTC+4.</li>
</ul>

<p>So a single "overnight: yes or no" answer would be wrong for this firm. If you want to hold trades for days, only the Crypto + TradFi products work. Our <a href="/best-swing-trading-prop-firms">swing trading prop firms</a> list covers firms where holding is the main design.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Trading costs, with a worked example</strong></h3>

<p>Hash Hedge charges maker 0.01% and taker 0.03% "on the total position size, including leverage, both when you open and when you close a position". Funding is variable and "exchanged between traders, not paid to the platform". Leverage is capped at 1:5: the homepage selector reads "Capped at 1:5 across all stages and all assets" on every stage, and the guide says "up to 1:5" (both read 2026-10-26). The "LEVERAGE X10" sale banner seen on 2026-10-23 is gone. A $100,000 notional position, which is 4x on a $25K account, costs $30 to open and $30 to close at the taker rate ($60 round trip), or $10 each way at the maker rate ($20). No spread figure is published for the perpetuals, so we cannot give a total trading cost per instrument. US Stocks charge 0.04% of the trade with a $1.00 minimum, per Hash Hedge's user guide (stock terminal page, read 2026-10-23).</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Rules that apply across products</strong></h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
  <li><strong>No time limit.</strong> The guide says a challenge "has no deadline".</li>
  <li><strong>Automation is out.</strong> Terms 12.2 bars "automated tools, bots, scripts, APIs, or software" that interact with the platform, and the platform is Hash Hedge's own terminal, so there is no MT4/MT5 EA route.</li>
  <li><strong>Hedging has a boundary.</strong> Holding long and short in the same instrument inside one account is permitted, but "Prohibited Hedging" means opposing positions across two or more challenges or accounts. Arbitrage and manipulation are banned.</li>
  <li><strong>No consistency rule found.</strong> We found none in the guide, Terms or Commercial Terms. That is not confirmation that none exists.</li>
  <li><strong>Identity checks come later.</strong> The guide says "there's no KYC" at purchase, but the Commercial Terms require identity verification before a Live Account and allow enhanced Re-Verification as a condition of any payout.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Payout speed in practice</strong></h2>

<p>A payout becomes available once the 14-day payout period has passed, the account is in profit and all positions are closed. The minimum is $100 and the maximum is $10,000 per account per 14 days. A new request cannot be made earlier than 14 days after your previous one was completed. That much is consistent, but the length of the cycle is not. The guide lists the payout cycle as "Every 14 days", while the Commercial Terms say the cycle ends on a payout day you select, which "may fall between fourteen (14) and sixty (60) calendar days from the opening of the first trade in that trading cycle" (both re-read 2026-10-26). Read that as day 14 at the earliest, not a guaranteed fortnightly schedule. You withdraw in USDT on one of six networks (ERC-20, TRC-20, BEP-20, Arbitrum, Optimism, Solana), and the homepage says Hash Hedge "sends the exact amount and covers the network fee".</p>

<p>On timing, the guide says funds are transferred "within 72 hours of submitting your withdrawal request". That is a ceiling, not an average, and Hash Hedge publishes no average payout time or payout statistics. The Commercial Terms also describe a payout processing guarantee: if a valid, complete request is not processed and paid within 72 hours, you are entitled to three times the payout. The guarantee applies only to requests "not subject to any Hold, investigation, Re-Verification or enhanced identity verification requirement". If one of those arises after submission, the 72-hour clock stops and a fresh 72 hours starts in full once the request is valid again. It also stops for delays outside the firm's reasonable control, including payment-network processing times and incorrect or unverified payout details. That makes the guarantee conditional on a clean request, which is a weaker promise than the homepage line "If your payout is delayed by more than 72 hours, we pay you triple".</p>
