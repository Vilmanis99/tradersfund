Ranked by highest profit split, then fewest days to first payout, then A–Z — only current products that allow news trading. Affiliate status does not move anyone. See the [news-trading filter](/prop-firms/news-trading).

Tradeify and Lucid Trading lead at 90%. Tradeify's Lightning Funded publishes 0 days to first payout; LucidFlex publishes 5 days. Maven is third at 80% / on-demand (0 days) via Buy Now, Pay Later. Instant, Mini, BNPL and Prediction Markets allow news; Standard 1/2/3 keep a 2-minute Red Folder window — funded Standard profit in that window is removed without ending the account (challenge phase still ends it). OMO funded news profit is capped at 0.50% of balance (Maven Terms Version 2026.3, effective / last amended 5 November 2026). Prediction Markets no longer wait 10 business days; the BNPL rank key is unchanged.

My Funded Futures Builder is next at 80% / 2 days. E8's 80% / 3 days is Signature Forex itself (news allowed); as of 2 November 2026 its live page still showed placeholder values and Classic Signature sale status was unclear. City Traders Imperium's 80% + 5 days is cross-product: 1-Step and 2-Step are 80% / 7 days; Instant Funding is 50% / 5 days and Direct Funding is 70% / 5 days — no single CTI product has both. FXIFY ranks on Two Phase Pro at 80% / 10 days. Crypto Fund Trader ranks tenth at 80% with no published first-payout day on Evaluation, Accelerated and Instant. Break is not sold to new buyers as of 6 November 2026. BrightFunded and FTMO do not qualify.
