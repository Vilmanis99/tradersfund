Alpha Capital is the one firm here that prints the Philippines by name, in the Asia section of its checkout-country guide. The other 13 made the list because their own restriction policies, read on 9 November 2026, leave the country off. That gets you to the shop window; KYC and payouts are still yours to test.

Rows follow our editorial score: FTMO first at 9.2, FundedNext next at 8.8. Expect a CFD-heavy table. Just 3 of the 14 sell futures (Apex Trader Funding, Topstep, Tradeify), against six of 15 on our [UAE list](/best-prop-firms-in-uae), and the lone 100% split belongs to Apex. One catch: Crypto Fund Trader qualifies on Match-Trader alone. Its Terms state "Match-Trader: No country restrictions," and the listing rests on that line alone, so don't assume its MT5 accounts are open to you.

Small budgets: Maven's $2,000 Standard 3-Step is $13 (read 27 October 2026), yet its 3% maximum loss leaves a $60 cushion; see [low-drawdown firms](/best-prop-firms-for-low-drawdown). Alpha Capital's $5,000 Pro 6% On-Demand is $27 (23 October), FundingPips' $5,000 2 Step Pro $29 (20 October). More in [cheapest prop firms](/cheapest-prop-firms).

BrightFunded ranks 11th of 14 at 7.8. Its help centre bars six countries, and the Philippines is not one. A 5K 2-Step Bright costs €47 (read 20 October 2026) with an 80% split; first payout after 30 days, then every 14.
