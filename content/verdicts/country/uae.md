Only one firm here names the United Arab Emirates outright: Alpha Capital lists it in its checkout-country guide. The other 14 publish first-party policies describing global or country-based availability that do not restrict the UAE (all read 9 November 2026). That is listing access — not a completed checkout, KYC approval, payout test or legal advice — so confirm your country at signup.

The order is our editorial score, nothing else. FTMO (9.2) and FundedNext (8.8) lead. Six of the 15 are futures firms — Apex Trader Funding, Lucid Trading, Take Profit Trader, Topstep, TradeDay and Tradeify; compare them on the [futures page](/best-futures-prop-firms). Apex is the only one listing a 100% split.

Cheapest way in: Maven's $2,000 Buy Now, Pay Later costs $5 upfront and $40 after you pass — $45 in total — while its $2,000 Standard 3-Step is $13 outright (prices read 27 October 2026). Topstep's 50K Combine is $49 a month, but the Standard path adds a $149 activation fee once you earn a funded account, so a two-month pass costs $247. More on the [cheapest prop firms](/cheapest-prop-firms).

BrightFunded sits 12th (7.8) and carries the UAE gotcha: its Terms say MetaTrader 5 is not intended for UAE residents or citizens, so you trade on DXTrade or cTrader. Entry starts at €47 for a 5,000 2-Step Bright account, with an 80% split; the first payout comes after 30 days, then every 14. Read the [BrightFunded review](/blog/bright-funded-prop-firm), pick your platform, then [open an account](/go/bright-funded?from=country-uae-verdict-bright-funded).
