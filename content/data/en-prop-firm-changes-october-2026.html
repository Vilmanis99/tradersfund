Introduction

Prop firms change their rules, payout terms, prices and promotions all the time, often without an announcement. This report lists the changes we could verify on the firms' own pages in October 2026, with the official source and the date we observed each one. Tables cover observations with firm-side or observation dates through 31 October 2026; a September change we only noticed in October is a footnote, is not counted in the October totals and is not in the CSV. Items still waiting to be published on our site, and items seen only on a firm page, are labelled. Unresolved items without a verbatim quote are under open questions and are not counted. Observations first dated 1 November 2026 are summarised after the cut-off, not in the tables.

Full row set through cut-off 31.10.2026: prop-firm-changes-october-2026.csv (56 rows; the September Topstep footnote is in the text below only, not in the CSV).

Methodology

  • Sources: first-party pages only (firm sites, help centres, terms, public pricing payloads). Closures may cite the firm's own domain response.
  • How we check: we load each firm page from a US-based server, with no VPN. Observation dates are when we saw the change; firm-side effective dates are printed only when the firm states one.
  • Live vs pending: LIVE = already reflected on tradersfundhub.com (as of 1 Nov 2026). PENDING = confirmed on the firm page but not yet reflected on our site. OBSERVATION = seen on the firm page and not yet added to our firm pages.
  • Promotions: codes are dated facts as the firm page showed them. None were checkout-verified. Codes marked US-only (unconfirmed) / regional may not apply outside the US-served page. Codes that ended per firm terms are listed as past.
  • What we don't do: no claims about payouts actually received, no fraud claims, no firm rankings.
  • Cut-off: 31.10.2026. Our 1 Nov promo-closure notes go under After cut-off when Observed = 1 Nov.
